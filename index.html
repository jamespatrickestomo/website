About
About Cortes

Cortes is a municipality in the province of Bohol, known for its welcoming communities and coastal charm. The Local Government Unit blends transparent, people-centered governance with a deep commitment to serving every Corteseño.

It continues to grow as a progressive, eco-tourism-driven municipality — anchored on inclusive development for every barangay of Cortes.

Beyond its coastal charm, Cortes is home to a growing local economy anchored on agriculture, fisheries, and small enterprise. Its barangays blend traditional livelihoods with emerging opportunities in eco-tourism and public service innovation.

Residents and visitors alike are drawn to the municipality's relaxed pace, scenic coastline, and strong sense of community. Local celebrations and barangay fiestas showcase the warmth and hospitality that define every Corteseño, making Cortes a genuine reflection of Bohol's rich culture and heritage.

History of Cortes

Cortes has a rich historical background rooted in its coastal and agricultural heritage. From a quiet farming and fishing community, it has grown into a progressive municipality that continues to honor its roots while embracing sustainable development for every barangay.

Our Mission

To deliver transparent, accountable, and people-centered governance — providing quality public services, sustainable livelihood opportunities, and a safe, resilient community for every Corteseño.

Our Vision

A progressive, eco-tourism-driven municipality of empowered and God-loving citizens, governed by transparent leadership and anchored on inclusive growth for every barangay of Cortes.

Our Roots
The Story of Cortes

The Municipality of Cortes is one of the 47 municipalities of the province of Bohol in the Philippines. Nestled along the banks of the Inabanga River, Cortes is named after the Spanish conquistador Hernán Cortés, a naming tradition from the colonial era that reflects the deep Spanish influence on the region.

Cortes was officially founded in 1862 during the Spanish colonial period. The municipality was carved from surrounding territories and established as a distinct political unit. Over the decades, it evolved from a quiet agricultural settlement into a thriving municipality with growing infrastructure and public services.

Spanish Colonial RootsLike many Bohol towns, Cortes' earliest church and plaza anchored the poblacion — a layout that still shapes the town center today.

The town played a quiet but significant role during the Philippine-American War and the Japanese occupation in World War II. The resiliency of the Corteseños — as its residents are called — helped the community rebuild and grow stronger through every challenge.

A Growing MunicipalityModern public infrastructure, like the new medical center, marks the latest chapter in the town's steady development.

Today, Cortes is rated as a High Functional LGU and continues to grow as a progressive municipality, with landmark developments such as the Governor Celestino Gallares Multi-Specialty Medical Center, which held its soft launch in September 2025 within the municipality.

"A community rooted in faith, culture, and resilience — the spirit of Cortes endures."

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles and aspirations of the Municipality of Cortes.

A progressive, peaceful, and prosperous Municipality of Cortes — with empowered Corteseños living in a healthy, safe, and sustainable environment, guided by transparent and accountable local governance.

To deliver efficient, equitable, and people-centered public services; to foster inclusive economic growth; to uphold the rule of law; and to protect and preserve the natural and cultural heritage of Cortes.

Privacy Policy

How this website handles the personal information you send us.

The Municipality of Cortes respects your privacy and handles personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

  • Contact Us form: your first name, last name, email address, the type of concern, and your message.
  • Live chat: the name you enter (if any) and the messages you send.

You can browse the rest of this website without giving any personal information. The site does not ask visitors to create an account.

How we use it

Your information is used only to read and respond to your inquiry and to refer it to the municipal office concerned. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Who can see it

Messages are stored on the municipal website's server and can be read only by authorized website administrators of the LGU.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information you sent through this website. Send your request through the Contact Us page.

Legal

Accessibility

Our aim is a website every Corteseño can use.

This website is built so it can be used on phones, tablets, and desktop computers, and by people who use a keyboard or assistive technology.

What we have done

  • The layout adjusts to small screens, and tables become easy-to-read cards on phones.
  • A "Skip to content" link and visible focus outlines support keyboard navigation.
  • Menus and buttons carry labels for screen readers.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this website is hard for you to use, or you need a document in another format, please let us know through the Contact Us page.
